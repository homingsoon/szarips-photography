About

I photograph people while they perform: on a theatre stage, behind a microphone, or dressed as someone else for a night.

I'm Syarif Lazuardi. You may also know my work as Szarips and Asaca Rei.

Most of the pictures here were made in low light, with whatever the stage or the room provided. I keep the darkness around a figure, so a gesture, a face or a raised hand has room to stand on its own.

Alongside stage and event work I make portraits, candid and documentary pictures, and personal projects. This site is an archive of that work, arranged as a sequence rather than a feed.
